# Art direction

**A playable 3D illustration**: clean shapes, large color masses, warm golden light, cool violet-blue shadows, a
painted sky, slightly watercolor-inspired surfaces and excellent readability.

- Characters follow Messenger-inspired *principles* (big head, short torso, long thin limbs, chunky shoes, graphic
  faces, fashion-first clothes) but are independently authored. They are brighter and cleaner than the world and carry
  a warm ink outline.
- The world sits one value step below characters in contrast and saturation; pigment breakup only lives on the world.
- Sky: gradient around the local up, painted cloud masses, soft sun glow. Fog uses the horizon color (aerial
  perspective).
- "Local golden hour": the sun follows the player's local frame so the whole planet stays in late-afternoon light.
- Never: photoreal humans, military operators, armor, procedural noise everywhere, color-only information.
