# UFO deployment (design for Phase 7 — TODO)

Deterministic orbit defined by the server (route, startTime, duration); clients derive the UFO position. The planet is
small enough to read from orbit, and the landmarks (giant tree, observatory, crystal fracture, sky shard, village,
lake) are placed to be recognizable from above. `UFO_JUMP` → radial fall with wind, camera-local speed lines, a small
trail and ground vs. water landing responses. No glider in the MVP.
